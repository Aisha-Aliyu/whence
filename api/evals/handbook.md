# Paid leave

Full-time staff get 25 days of paid leave each year, plus public holidays. Up to 5 unused days carry over to the next year, and those days expire on 31 March.

# Sick leave

Tell your manager before 10:00 on the first day you are sick. From the fourth sick day in a row, you need a doctor's note.

# Expenses

Submit expense claims within 30 days, with a receipt. Meals while travelling are covered up to 40 euros a day. Taxis are covered only after 22:00 or when no train runs.

# Remote work

Staff may work remotely up to three days a week. Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in the office's time zone.

# Equipment

Every new starter gets a laptop and a monitor. Report lost or stolen equipment to IT within 24 hours.

# Security

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Two-factor authentication is required for email and the code repository. Report phishing with the Report button in your email app, and never forward a phishing email.

# Parental leave

Birth parents get 26 weeks of paid leave. Other parents get 8 weeks, taken within the child's first year.

# Learning budget

Everyone has 1,000 euros a year for courses, books and conferences. Ask your manager before booking anything that costs more than 300 euros.
